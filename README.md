# StockSplit AI

### Intelligent On-Chain Credit & Risk Management for Solana

> **Smart collateral. Transparent credit. Intelligent risk.**

StockSplit AI is a mobile-first Web3 application exploring on-chain collateral management, credit positions, and intelligent risk analysis on the Solana network.

Built with a focus on transparency, real blockchain data, and user-friendly risk management.

---

## Overview

Managing on-chain credit can be complex and difficult to understand.

StockSplit AI is designed to provide a simplified experience for monitoring and understanding:

- On-chain collateral
- Credit positions
- Borrowing exposure
- Loan-to-value ratios
- Liquidation risk
- Market scenarios

The goal is to make on-chain credit more transparent, understandable, and risk-aware.

---

| Data | Source |

| Transactions | Solana Network |
| Position Data | On-Chain Program |
| Collateral | On-Chain Vault |
| Risk Metrics | Position Calculations |
